## Setting and running dates
- How things go before the date sets how ready she is on it. Look at the whole "funnel" (first impression, then dates, then three or more dates and intimacy), not just the date itself. [v3960432938843600031]
- Each stage has a "dead zone" where things stop moving and end: never getting to a first date, never getting to intimacy, or her vanishing after three dates. Work out which stage you're stalling at. [v3984972070877741265]
- Don't run an "interview date" of back-and-forth questions with no emotion or tension. "You'll never hear from her again." [v4008950092802932533]
- Answer her questions playfully instead of reciting facts. Early on she cares more about how the conversation feels than about your job details. [v3998652338042291352]
- Be willing to step into her silly, playful world in conversation and show you can "hang" with it, instead of keeping everything serious. [v3970464003649949985]
- If she vents about something on the date, lean in and play along with her energy rather than staying neutral. Bonding over a shared playful gripe builds excitement. [v3975356196063689622]

## Escalation & leading
- Make "executive decisions" as often as you can, from where to eat up to bigger plans. Decisiveness shows competence. [v3966140502176689621]
- Take the burden of planning off her: "We're going to do that, we're going to do this." Many women are relieved not to have to decide. [v3966877903995851791]
- When she flirts, don't jump to matching it or turning it sexual. Stay composed and let her keep investing. [v3977495591910655994]
- Avoid raising the stakes too early, such as talk of a trip or bigger spending, to speed things along. It reads as overcompensating. [v3947394613283119777]
